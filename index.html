Saving Children from the Streets Since 1992

Originally named Full Proof Mission, Chisomo was founded by Aaron Chilunjika after witnessing children being exploited on the streets. What began as classes under a tree has grown into a comprehensive program serving vulnerable children across Zambia.

Registered with the Zambian government in 1994, we operate as an interdenominational para-church organization, providing both intervention and prevention services to break the cycle of street life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience to volunteer?

No experience is necessary! We provide training and support. Whether you're skilled or simply caring, there's a role for you. Professional expertise is always welcome but not required.

What about background checks?

Yes, all volunteers working with children undergo background checks. This is to ensure the safety and protection of the children in our care. This is a non-negotiable requirement.

How much does sponsoring a child cost?

Child sponsorship is flexible and customizable. You can choose the level of support that fits your budget - from partial support to full sponsorship. Contact us for specific details.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Yes! Chisomo Centers is a registered charitable para-church organization. All donations are tax-deductible. We provide receipts for all contributions.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes! We have opportunities for remote volunteers - from administration and fundraising to IT support and online mentoring. Contact us to discuss remote options.

How often do you report on fund usage?

We provide annual impact reports detailing how funds are used. Sponsors receive regular updates about their sponsored child. We believe in complete transparency.

What We Offer

Our Drop-in Center serves as the first point of contact for many street children. It's a safe, non-judgmental space where children can access essential services without any commitment, helping them take the first steps toward a better life.

Daily Meals

Nutritious breakfast and lunch provided every day

Hygiene Facilities

Access to showers, toilets, and clean clothing

Basic Medical Care

First aid, wound treatment, and health screening

Counseling Support

One-on-one sessions with trained counselors

Recreation Activities

Sports, games, and creative activities

Trust Building

Building relationships and confidence with staff

Our Approach

We understand that trust must be earned. Our Drop-in Center operates with an open-door policy, allowing children to come and go freely while gradually building relationships. This low-barrier approach helps children feel safe and respected, often leading them to engage more deeply with our other programs.

Breaking the Cycle Through Education

Education is the most powerful tool for breaking the cycle of poverty and street life. Our comprehensive education program addresses the unique needs of children who have missed significant schooling, providing both formal education and practical vocational training.

Formal Education

Primary and secondary school enrollment and support

Catch-up Classes

Remedial education for children behind in their studies

Vocational Training

Carpentry, tailoring, mechanics, and computer skills

Art & Music

Creative expression through arts and cultural programs

Digital Literacy

Basic computer skills and technology training

Job Readiness

Interview skills, CV writing, and workplace etiquette

Our Approach

We recognize that every child learns differently and at their own pace. Our teachers are specially trained to work with children who have experienced trauma and educational gaps. We provide individualized learning plans, small class sizes, and patient, encouraging instruction.

Reuniting Families

Family reintegration is at the heart of our mission. We believe that children thrive best in family environments. Our comprehensive reintegration program carefully prepares both children and families for successful reunification, addressing the root causes that led to family separation.

Family Tracing

Locating and contacting children's families

Assessment

Evaluating family readiness and home environment

Family Counseling

Addressing conflicts and building communication

Home Visits

Gradual reintroduction and monitored reunification

Economic Support

Income-generating assistance for families

Follow-up Care

Regular monitoring and ongoing family support

The Reintegration Process

Our process takes 6-12 months and includes thorough preparation, counseling, gradual home visits, and extended follow-up support. We work to address economic challenges, family dynamics, and community acceptance to ensure lasting success.

Success Metrics

200+ Children Reintegrated
85% Successful Reunifications
2 Years Average Follow-up Period

Women's Prevention Program

Prevention is Better Than Cure

Our Women's Prevention Program works with at-risk families before children end up on the streets. By empowering mothers and caregivers with income-generating skills and business support, we address the root economic causes that often lead families to send children to work on the streets.

Microfinance Loans

Small business loans with mentorship support

Business Training

Entrepreneurship and financial literacy skills

Skills Development

Tailoring, hairdressing, and other marketable skills

Support Groups

Peer support and community building

Business Mentorship

Ongoing guidance and business development

Family Counseling

Support for healthy family relationships

How It Works

Women receive training in business skills and a chosen trade, followed by a small loan to start or expand their business. Our team provides ongoing mentorship and support. As family income increases, the risk of children being sent to the streets dramatically decreases.
